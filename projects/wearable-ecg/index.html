CASE STUDY 02 · MEDICAL DEVICES / RESEARCH

Wearable flexible ECG sensing system

A skin-adaptive cardiac sensing concept connecting flexible device design, signal acquisition and mobile visualization.

RoleProject member
Period2022 — 2023
SupportShanghai innovation programme
OutcomeUtility model patent

01 / PROBLEM

The challenge

Conventional short-term ECG measurement does not fully address continuous, comfortable monitoring outside clinical settings. The project explored a wearable form that could remain close to the body, acquire cardiac signals and make the information accessible through a connected mobile experience.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My contribution

  1. 01Participated in the design and development of the wearable ECG detector.
  2. 02Contributed to the flexible device form and integrated product concept.
  3. 03Supported ECG signal acquisition and the wireless connection to a mobile interface.
  4. 04Worked with the companion Mini Program concept for ECG settings, history and waveform visualization.
  5. 05Contributed to project research, technical presentation and documentation.
  6. 06Completed utility model patent application and specification work with the team.

03 / SYSTEM

System concept

01

Sense

Flexible electrodes acquire the body-surface ECG signal.

02

Condition

The circuit prepares the low-amplitude physiological signal for use.

03

Filter

Signal processing reduces noise and preserves the cardiac waveform.

04

Transmit

The device sends ECG data wirelessly to a mobile terminal.

05

Visualize

The companion interface presents waveform and historical information.

06

Document

Design evidence is consolidated into research and patent materials.

04 / EVIDENCE

Design evidence

05 / RESULT

Outcome

The project produced a wearable ECG detector concept with wireless mobile connectivity and received support from the Shanghai Municipal College Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Training Program. The work also resulted in an approved utility model patent. Clinical accuracy or medical diagnostic performance is not claimed by the available project evidence.
