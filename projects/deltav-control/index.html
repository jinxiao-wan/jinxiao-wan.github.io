The project

The thesis studies temperature–flow cascade control and a feed-ratio follower, alongside system architecture, I/O, startup sequencing, alarms and operator displays.

My contribution

  • Analyzed process requirements and compared control structures.
  • Designed control modules, startup sequence and alarm logic.
  • Developed operator displays and reviewed simulated trends.

Outcome & scope

The original work is theoretical and simulation-level research. The new browser demo makes cascade, ratio, disturbance and trip/reset behavior visible through a normalized teaching model. It does not reproduce a commissioned plant or a native DeltaV configuration.

Full designs, source files and development details are in the project repository.
