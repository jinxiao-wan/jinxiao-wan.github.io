CASE STUDY 03 · CONTROL / INDUSTRIAL SYSTEMS

DeltaV nitration process control

A DCS-based control design for an adiabatic nitration process, integrating regulatory control, startup sequencing, protection and operator supervision.

RoleIndependent thesis project
Period18 weeks · 2024
PlatformEmerson DeltaV DCS
ScopeControl · HMI · safety logic

01 / PROBLEM

The challenge

An industrial nitration process must keep interacting flow, temperature and operating-state variables within safe and productive ranges. The design therefore needed more than isolated PID loops: it required coordinated control strategies, a repeatable startup sequence, protection interlocks, alarm handling and clear operator visibility.

02 / OWNERSHIP

My contribution

  1. 01Analysed the nitration process, operating requirements and key input–output variables.
  2. 02Compared control structures and designed complex-loop strategies for the process.
  3. 03Configured control modules and alarm behavior in DeltaV Control Studio.
  4. 04Designed the startup sequence and protection-interlock logic.
  5. 05Built operator displays, alarm views and historical trends in DeltaV Live.
  6. 06Tested the configuration through simulation, analysed trends and documented the complete design.

06 / RESULT

Outcome

The thesis delivered a complete DeltaV control design covering system configuration, control logic, startup sequencing, protection interlocks, alarms, trends and operator graphics. Simulation and trend analysis were used to compare behavior and refine the selected control approach. No plant-production improvement percentage is claimed because the available evidence is simulation- and configuration-based.
